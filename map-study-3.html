2 · The map — every spec, one canvas

Three ways to give all 70 a position. Every dot is a spec: class color, class glyph, hover for the name, tap for the profile. No axis orders by quality — positions are identity, never rank.

M1 · The role compass structural — Data tier

How to read it: four poles — Damage, Tank, Healer, Support. A spec sits at its job; a spec with two jobs sits between them. The crowd at the Damage pole is the honest truth of the roster.

M2 · The style field drafted axes

How to read it: rightward = damage arrives in bursts and windows; leftward = it flows steadily. Upward = your value lands on the group; downward = on your own target. Drafted from families, roles, and player text — same disclosure as G1.

M3 · The family starmap site taxonomy

How to read it: eight constellations, one per playstyle family — the site's own taxonomy. Specs cluster with their kin; neighbors play alike. Family grouping is editorial, as the Atlas already says.

Dot color follows the class but is not separable at 21 hues (measured, round 1); the glyph inside each dot and the hover name carry identity. Class emphasis interaction lands once a direction wins.
