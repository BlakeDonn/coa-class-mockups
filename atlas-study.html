1 · Chrome & the altitude fork

The ruled phone chrome (grammar §6b) applied to the Atlas shell, plus three ways to offer the landing altitudes: Guide · the 21 classes · all 70 specs. At phone width the demo's top row becomes the ruled icon chrome; on desktop the labeled tabs stay. The phone topbar drops the ⚖ Workbench button — the bottom tray already appears the moment a spec is benched (proposal).

2 · The Class Atlas — 21 cards

The class as the browsing unit. All 21, real roster data, alphabetical. Tap a spec anywhere to open its full profile.

3 · The identity map

Reading the whole roster at once. The axes are demand from the research — never quality; nothing here ranks. Values carry Data · Players · Inference tiers and are relative among researched CoA specs.

A · The constellation wall

How to read it: every spec is one shape on the same six spokes — the farther a shape reaches toward a spoke, the more of that demand the spec asks of you. Similar shapes play alike. Tap any shape for the full profile.

Six axes from research v2. Sequencing joins as a seventh spoke with research v3 — an unresearched value renders as a hollow spoke, never as zero.

B · The field

Dot color follows the class, but 21 class colors cannot be told apart reliably (Ranger and Witch Hunter green differ by ΔE 0.3 — measured, not eyeballed). Color never carries identity alone here: tap a class crest above to light its specs and name them, tap a dot for its profile.

C · The muster board

How to read it: columns are jobs, rows are where you stand. A spec appears at every post it can hold. Purely structural — roster facts only, no demand values, so the family lens is the only filter that applies.

Everything on this page is PROPOSED. Rulings land in atlas-page-grammar.md, same format as the class-page contract. The shipped Atlas (index.html) is untouched by this study.
