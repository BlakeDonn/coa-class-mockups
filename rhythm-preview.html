Rhythm study — three candidate visual forms

The seal (ruled: engine + one tension + verbs) answers "what is this class's thing." The weakest-answered question is "what will my hands and eyes be doing." This page tests three forms for that job, on real researched Cultist and Tinker data. Ability names come from the snapshot (Data tier). Every shape is our synthesis (Inference tier), and says so.

R3 Round 3 — "where your eyes live," drawn as your actual screen

A stylized HUD instead of dots or sentences: party frames, nameplates, the ground, your bar. Bright means your attention lives there. Dim means it exists, but you rarely look. Each card keeps its one-line caption; the picture shows, the sentence tells.

What it buys
  • Shows the literal thing you'll be staring at, in game furniture players already know.
  • Healer vs caster vs engineer separates instantly.
  • One shared scene for all 70 specs; only the lighting changes.
What it risks
  • It's a claim about the game's real UI; if it looks wrong, trust drops.
  • Small screens will squeeze the fine details.
  • More seductive than dots, so its Inference label matters more.

What it buys
  • Answers "what will I be doing" in one glance.
  • The three lives look genuinely different: spike vs wave vs stack.
  • Reuses the seal's language (hold/cross carries straight over).
What it risks
  • Anything timeline-shaped can read as a rotation guide.
  • 70 strips means 70 authored drawings, each an editorial claim.
  • Thin-evidence specs would get invented topologies.

B Attention row — where your eyes live

Four fixed channels for every spec in the game: your own bar, the enemy, the ground, the party. Coarse on purpose. Cheap, uniform, comparable across all 70 specs.

Inference Relative emphasis, inferred from researched mechanics. Not measured, and never comparable as "difficulty."
What it buys
  • One glance separates a placement class from a bar-watching class.
  • Uniform: works identically for all 70 specs, thin evidence included.
  • Cheap to author and cheap to keep honest.
What it risks
  • Says nothing about rhythm, windows, or failure.
  • Dots are pips again: they can read as ratings.
  • Four channels flatten hybrid attention (mouse vs eyes vs ears).
